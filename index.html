Privacy Policy — Cozy Sort

Developer: Games Lab
Effective date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cozy Sort (the “Game”) handles information when you play. Games Lab aims to collect only the technical and gameplay information needed to understand the Game and keep it stable.

Information handled by the Game

Firebase Analytics — optional

Firebase Analytics is disabled until you choose to share gameplay analytics. If enabled, the Game sends product events such as level starts and completions, hints, undo use, result-screen actions, star results, move counts, level identifiers and an ephemeral random session identifier. Firebase Analytics also processes an app-instance identifier, app lifecycle and session information, app version, device model, operating-system information and approximate geography derived from a masked IP address. Games Lab disables Android Advertising ID collection and advertising-personalization signals for Analytics. Games Lab does not configure a user ID, request your name or email address, or send free-form text through Analytics events.

You may continue playing without sharing Analytics. You may also enable or disable Analytics later from the Game’s Privacy Settings. Disabling it stops future Analytics event collection by the Game.

Firebase Crashlytics — active by default

The Game uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect crashes and technical errors. Crashlytics may process crash traces, technical installation identifiers, session identifiers, timestamps, app version, device model and technical device information, operating-system information, and whether a device is rooted or jailbroken. Games Lab does not add custom logs containing names, email addresses or other user-provided personal information.

Local game data

Game progress, settings and your Analytics preference are stored locally on your device. Cozy Sort currently does not provide user accounts or cloud saves.

Why this information is used

  • Optional Analytics information is used to understand gameplay, progression and product quality.
  • Crashlytics information is used to diagnose crashes, fix defects and improve stability.
  • Local data is used to preserve progress and preferences on your device.

Service provider and international processing

Games Lab uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Google processes information on Games Lab’s behalf under its applicable service terms and may process it on global infrastructure. You can read Firebase’s privacy and security information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising

This version of the policy does not describe advertising collection because advertising SDKs are not currently enabled in the Game. Games Lab will update this policy and the Game’s disclosures before enabling advertising or related consent flows.

Retention and deletion

Games Lab keeps Analytics information only for the period configured in the Firebase project and only while it is useful for the purposes described above. Firebase states that Crashlytics crash traces and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins. Local progress and preferences remain on your device until you clear the Game’s data or uninstall it.

You may request information about deletion by contacting Games Lab. Because Cozy Sort does not use accounts or configure a user ID, Games Lab may be unable to identify records belonging to one specific player. Disabling Analytics prevents future Analytics events from being sent by the Game.

Security

Information sent to Firebase is transmitted using encrypted connections. Firebase states that Crashlytics data is encrypted at rest. Games Lab limits Firebase project access to people who need it to operate and improve the Game.

Changes to this policy

Games Lab may update this policy when Cozy Sort’s data practices or service providers change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when that happens.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Games Lab at info.games.lab.policies@gmail.com.
